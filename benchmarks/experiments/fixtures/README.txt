e2-custody-ceiling-n1.log is a real QEMU development trace with capacity 1,
WORK=4000, INTERFERENCE=40000, SAMPLES=1, ARMv7-M PROFILE and ceiling enabled.
It supplies handoff, timeout and direct-delivery records for acceptance-gate
tests, including regrant/resend before the old custodian's free call returns.
It is a parser fixture, not the published quantitative result set. Published
results are regenerated from the source commit recorded in their manifests.
